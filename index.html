Kandoli Mountain Beekeeping

From the forested highlands of Northern Malawi, Kandoli Mountain Beekeeping produces raw, natural honey harvested using sustainable and community-driven practices. Our honey is unprocessed, chemical-free, and rich in natural enzymes and nutrients.

We work directly with local beekeepers, supporting rural livelihoods while protecting the forests that give our honey its distinctive mountain character.

About Kandoli Mountain Beekeeping

Who We Are

Kandoli Mountain Beekeeping is a community-focused honey producer based in Northern Malawi. We combine traditional beekeeping knowledge with responsible environmental practices to produce high-quality mountain honey.

How We Work

Our honey is harvested from forest-based hives placed carefully to preserve biodiversity. We avoid industrial processing methods and instead use natural filtering techniques that maintain flavor, nutrients, and authenticity.

Quality and Sustainability

Every batch undergoes quality checks to ensure purity and consistency. By promoting sustainable hive placement and responsible forest use, we support long-term environmental balance and rural economic growth.
